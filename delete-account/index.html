Delete Account

First, try to delete your account directly in the WalletWise app. It's faster and more secure.

How to delete your account in the app:
  • Open the WalletWise app
  • Go to Settings → Account
  • Tap “Delete Account”
  • Confirm the deletion

Note: This action is irreversible. After deletion, all personal data and saved items will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered (unless retention is required by law).

If you can't delete your account in the app

Contact the developer — we'll help delete your account manually. Please include your account email, platform (iOS/Android), and optionally your reason for deletion.

Email: trofimpanasikapps@gmail.com

We usually respond within 1–3 business days.
